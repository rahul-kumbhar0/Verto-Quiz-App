Okay so, every AI company is suddenly launching agents. OpenAI just gave its agents their own computer and browser. But what even is an agent? Here's the simplest definition. An agent is AI that uses tools, in a loop, until a goal is done. A chatbot answers once and stops. An agent keeps going. Say you tell it, find me three internships and save them in a sheet. It thinks, I need to search. It acts, and opens the browser. It looks at the results. Then it decides the next step. Search again, open a page, write to the sheet.

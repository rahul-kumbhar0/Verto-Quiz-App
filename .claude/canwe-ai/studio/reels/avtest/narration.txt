Let's design a URL shortener. You paste a long link, and get back seven characters. So how does that work? First, the database. Every short code is one row: the code, the long URL, and a click counter. Next, the redirect. Your browser asks for the code, the server looks up the row, and answers with a 301. Then, scale. Now imagine a link goes viral. Ten thousand requests hit the same row, so a cache in front of Postgres answers most of them.

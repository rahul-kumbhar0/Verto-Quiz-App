Your coding interview is recording more than your code.
So how do companies catch ChatGPT answers?
First, the paste.
Platforms log every paste and every tab switch.
Forty clean lines in one frame is not how humans type.
Next, your typing rhythm.
Real coders type, pause and fix.
AI answers show a long pause, then a sudden burst of perfect code.
Then, the comparison.
CodeSignal checks your answer against every submission and solutions posted online, then gives you one suspicion score.
On a live call, interviewers watch your eyes drift to the same spot, and the long hmm while the answer loads.
But their best trick is one follow-up question.
Why this approach? What breaks if the input doubles?
The AI wrote the code, but it can't explain it for you.
So use AI to prepare, not to perform.

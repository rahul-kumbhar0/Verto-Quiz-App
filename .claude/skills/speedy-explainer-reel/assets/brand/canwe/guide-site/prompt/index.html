STEP 01

The prompt (copy-paste)

Here is my resume (attached). Answer in 3 short parts: 1) The 5 job titles I'm actually qualified for RIGHT NOW in India (not dream roles), with one line each on why. 2) The 3 skills stopping me from the next level for those roles, and how I could learn each in about 2 weeks. 3) The exact keywords recruiters search for these roles that are MISSING from my resume. Only use what's in my resume. Don't invent experience or skills.
STEP 02

How to use it

  1. Open Claude (claude.ai) or ChatGPT. The free plans work.
  2. Attach your resume PDF (or paste the text).
  3. Paste the prompt above and send.

🔒 Before uploading, remove your phone number and home address. The AI doesn't need them to judge your fit.

STEP 03

The answer from the reel

Sample fresher resume: B.Tech CSE 2026, Python, basic SQL, Pandas, Power BI, a 2-month data internship, 3 projects. Claude answered:

🎯
Jobs he fits now: Data Analyst (fresher) · Business Analyst Trainee · MIS / Reporting Analyst · Junior Python Developer · Power BI Developer (entry)
🧱
Skills holding him back: Advanced SQL (joins, window functions) · Excel (pivot tables, XLOOKUP) · Statistics basics
🔎
Missing keywords: data cleaning · dashboards · KPIs · ETL · stakeholder reporting

Your answer will be different: it reads your resume. Treat it as a strong second opinion, not the final word.

STEP 04

3 follow-up prompts

1 · Add the missing keywords (honestly)

Rewrite my resume bullet points to naturally include the missing keywords I GENUINELY have experience with. For any keyword I don't have, don't add it: list it separately so I know what to learn.

2 · Close the gap in 14 days

Make me a 14-day plan to learn [skill], 1 hour a day, using free resources. End with one small project I can add to my resume and GitHub.

3 · Prepare for the interview

For the role [job title], ask me the 5 interview questions I'm most likely to get with this resume, one at a time. After each answer, tell me how to make it stronger using my real projects.

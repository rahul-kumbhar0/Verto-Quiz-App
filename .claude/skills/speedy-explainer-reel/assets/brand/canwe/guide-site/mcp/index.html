Plug 5 apps into Claude. Setup for all.

These connections use MCP (Model Context Protocol): think of it as a USB port for AI. The company runs a small "server", you paste its link into Claude once, and Claude can use that app for you. Each one below has the link, the steps, and the honest catch.

How to add a connection (once)

  1. Open claude.ai (or the Claude desktop app) → Settings → Connectors.
  2. Tap Add custom connector, paste the link from the section below, save.
  3. Sign in to that app when it asks (that's how it knows it's you), then start a new chat.

💡 Plan limits change: as of late 2026, several guides say the free Claude plan allows one custom connector, with more on paid plans. Check what your Settings page shows.

01 · ZOMATO

Order food from inside Claude

Zomato's own MCP server (launched Oct 2025): Claude searches restaurants, reads menus, builds your cart, and you pay by scanning a UPI QR yourself.

https://mcp-server.zomato.com/mcp
Find a veg biryani near me under ₹250 with good ratings and build the cart. Show me the cart before checkout.

⚠️ The catch: Zomato labels its MCP setup as being for testing, so expect rough edges. Always check the cart and the amount before you pay.

02 · SWIGGY

Fill your Instamart cart

Swiggy runs official MCP servers for Food, Instamart (40,000+ products) and Dineout (launched Jan 2026). Claude can plan and fill a grocery cart from one message.

⏳ Request access (Swiggy) 📘 Swiggy MCP docs
Plan one week of simple hostel breakfasts and fill my Instamart cart with what I need. Keep it under ₹800 and show me the list first.

⏳ The catch: it's early access — production use needs approval via the form above. Early reports also said payment was cash on delivery only.

03 · ZERODHA

Check your portfolio's risk

Zerodha's official Kite MCP is free. After you log in to Kite, Claude can read your holdings, positions and P&L and explain where your risk sits.

https://mcp.kite.trade/mcp
Look at my holdings. Which sector or single stock is my biggest concentration risk? Explain in simple words. Don't suggest any trades.

📌 The catch: this is analysis, not advice. Zerodha describes the hosted server as read-only (GTT orders aside), but one review saw order tools listed. Never ask an AI to place trades for you.

04 · GOVT OF INDIA

Official stats, straight from the source

The Ministry of Statistics (MoSPI / National Statistics Office) launched India's first government MCP server in Feb 2026 on its eSankhyiki portal. It's free and needs no login.

https://mcp.mospi.gov.in/
Using the official MoSPI data, what is the latest CPI inflation figure, which month is it for, and what was it a year earlier? Cite the dataset.

🧪 The catch: it's a beta with a limited set of datasets. Official setup steps: datainnovation.mospi.gov.in — use those if the link above changes. UPSC aspirants: always note the date of a stat.

05 · GMAIL

Never miss a recruiter reply

Gmail is one of Claude's built-in connectors (with Calendar and Drive): no link needed, just Settings → Connectors → Gmail and sign in with Google.

Search my Gmail for every reply from recruiters or companies in the last 30 days. List interview invites, assignments and deadlines in a table, then draft a short follow-up for anyone I haven't answered.

✋ The catch: Claude reads and drafts — it doesn't send. You review every draft and press send yourself. Sources disagree on which plans include it; check your Connectors page.

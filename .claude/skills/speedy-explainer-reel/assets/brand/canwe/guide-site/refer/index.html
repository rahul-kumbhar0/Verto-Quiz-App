Get a referral from a stranger. My exact templates.

One hiring platform's data from 4.5 million applications: referred candidates were 7× more likely to be hired than job-board applicants. Here's how to get one without knowing anyone.

STEP 01

Pick the exact job first

Open the company's own careers page, find the role, and copy the job link and job ID. Never ask "any openings?". Asking for one specific job makes it easy for someone to say yes.

STEP 02

Find the right person on LinkedIn

  1. Search the company name, then open People.
  2. Filter Current company = the company and School = your college. Alumni share something with you already.
  3. Pick people in the same team or role (an SDE for an SDE job), ideally 1–5 years of experience.
  4. Message 3–5 people, not 50. Like or comment on one of their posts first if you can.
STEP 03

Message templates (copy-paste)

1 · The 3-line referral ask

Hi [Name]! I'm [Your name], final-year [branch] at [college] (your alma mater 👋). I'm applying for [Role] at [Company]: [job link] (Job ID: [ID]). I built [project] using [tech that matches the job], resume attached. Would you be open to referring me? No pressure at all, thank you!

2 · If you have no college connection

Hi [Name]! I loved your post on [topic]. I'm [Your name], a [degree] fresher. I'm applying for [Role] at [Company]: [job link]. My [project / internship] matches the role ([1 skill], [1 skill]). Resume attached. If you think I'm a fit, would you consider referring me? Totally okay if not 🙏

3 · Polite follow-up (after 4–5 days)

Hi [Name], just following up on my message about the [Role] role ([job link]). I know you're busy, so no worries at all if it's not possible. Thanks for reading either way!

4 · Thank-you (always send it)

Thank you so much for the referral, [Name]! I'll keep you posted on how it goes. Really appreciate you taking the time 🙏
STEP 04

Do's and don'ts

  • ✓
    Send the job link and job ID, not "any openings?"
  • ✓
    Attach or link a one-page resume that matches the job's keywords.
  • ✓
    Use their name and keep it under 5 lines.
  • ✓
    Follow up once, after 4–5 days. Never more than twice.
  • ✗
    "Hi sir", "Hello dear", or messaging only "??"
  • ✗
    Copy-pasting the same message to 100 people at once.

💡 Many companies pay employees a referral bonus when the person they refer gets hired, so a good candidate is genuinely helping them. Make it easy: the right job link, a matching resume, and a short message.

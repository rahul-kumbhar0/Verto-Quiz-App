STEP 03

Add a YouTube connector to Claude

The skill pack doesn't upload. A YouTube MCP server (connector) does, using YOUR Google login from step 2. Point it at your client_secret file, add it to Claude, then sign in once with the channel-owner Google account. The token stays on your computer.

  • 1
    Pick an open-source YouTube MCP server that supports OAuth uploads, for example pauling-ai/youtube-mcp-server (MIT). These are small community projects: read the code or README before you give it your login.
  • 2
    Follow its README: put your client_secret JSON where it says, then add the server to Claude (Claude Desktop config or Claude Code MCP settings).
  • 3
    Sign in once with the Google account that owns your channel. Ask Claude "check my YouTube connection" to confirm it works.
accounts.google.com
claude-youtube wants to access your Google Account
yourchannel@gmail.com
  • Manage your YouTube account
  • See your YouTube Analytics reports
CANCELALLOW

The one-time sign-in. You may first see "Google hasn't verified this app": it's YOUR app in testing, so click Continue.

STEP 04

The prompt, and the honest catches

Make a YouTube Short from my latest reel: use /yt-script to pick the best hook, /yt-package to check the title and thumbnail, /yt-seo for the description and tags. Then upload it to my channel as PRIVATE and give me the Studio link. Don't publish.
studio.youtube.com · Content → Shorts
Visibility
PrivateWhere every API upload lands
UnlistedAnyone with the link
PublicEveryone can watch
SAVE

YouTube Studio → Content → your Short → Visibility → Public → Save. This is the one step Claude never does.

🔒
Uploads land private. YouTube keeps uploads from new, unverified Google projects private until the project passes an audit. You review in Studio and press publish.
🔢
About 6 uploads a day. The free quota is 10,000 units/day and one upload costs 1,600.
⏳
Re-sign-in weekly. While your Google app is in "Testing", the login expires after 7 days.
🧠
You're still the editor. Watch every Short before it goes public. Your channel, your name.

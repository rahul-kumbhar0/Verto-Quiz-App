STEP 01

The exam upload cheat sheet

⚠️ These are the commonly used limits from recent official notices. Exams tweak them, so always re-check your own form's instructions before uploading.

SSC (CGL, CHSL, MTS…)
PhotoJPEG, recent, no cap/glasses20–50 KB
SignatureJPEG, black/blue ink on white10–20 KB
IBPS (PO, Clerk, RRB…)
Photo200×230 px JPEG20–50 KB
Signature140×60 px JPEG10–20 KB
Left thumb impression240×240 px20–50 KB

UPSC (OTR / CSE and others)
PhotoJPG20 KB–300 KB

Safe rule: keep a resume under 200 KB and it uploads everywhere.

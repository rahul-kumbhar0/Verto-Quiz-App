Delete your TV's ads. Safely.

cleanyourtv is the free, open-source project from the reel: a coding agent like Claude Code connects to your TV over Wi-Fi, switches off the ad apps, sponsored rows and trackers, and installs a clean home screen. Nothing is uninstalled, and one command restores everything.

STEP 01

Check your TV first

✅
Works: Android TV and Google TV (the clean launcher needs Android 8.0+). Most-tested brands in the repo: TCL (76 models), Sony (40), Xiaomi (32).
🟡
Fire TV: only the debloat half works; Fire OS won't let a third-party launcher take over Home.
❌
Samsung (Tizen), LG (webOS), Roku, Vizio, Hisense VIDAA: not supported, there's no ADB. For these, the project's site has a page on turning off tracking with just the remote: cleanyour.tv/not-android.

Not sure which system your TV runs? Settings → About usually says "Android TV" or "Google TV".

STEP 02

What you need

  1. A computer on the same Wi-Fi as the TV.
  2. A coding agent: Claude Code (what I use), or Codex / Gemini CLI. No agent? The repo's commands can be run by hand with just Python 3.
  3. On the TV: Settings → About → tap Build 7 times (unlocks developer options), then turn on USB debugging (and the network/ADB debugging toggle if your TV has one).
  4. No laptop at all? The community-built Clean Your TV phone app does the same job from your phone, linked in the repo's README.
⭐ Open the repo on GitHub
STEP 03

The one prompt (~15 minutes)

  1. Clone the repo and open the folder in Claude Code:
git clone https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv.git cd cleanyourtv claude
  1. Open the repo's PROMPT.md, paste it into the agent, and fill in your TV model. Add what you want to keep:
My TV is a [brand + model, e.g. TCL 55C645]. Follow PROMPT.md: back up first, then debloat, then install the clean launcher. Keep Netflix, Prime Video and Hotstar. Ask me before disabling anything you're not sure about.

The agent backs up your TV's current state, switches the junk off in small batches, installs the clean launcher and sets it as Home. Your model's exact list lives in the repo's examples/ folder (170+ TV models from 200+ contributors).

STEP 04

The safety rules (please read)

🔁
Nothing is uninstalled. Apps are disabled with pm disable-user. Undo everything with tools/restore.sh; a backup record is written to ~/.cleanyourtv/ before any change.
🏠
Order matters: disable the stock launcher only AFTER the new home screen is installed and working, or Home can land somewhere broken.
🔐
Turn debugging OFF when you're done. Network ADB is unencrypted on port 5555; on some TVs anyone on your Wi-Fi could connect while it's on.
🔄
Firmware updates can bring the junk back. Re-run the check afterwards.
⚠️
Know the risks: disabling the wrong package can break a feature you use (that's why it goes in small batches with restore ready), and changing a TV's software may affect its warranty or support. Your TV, your call.

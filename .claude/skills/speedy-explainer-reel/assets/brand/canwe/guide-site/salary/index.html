STEP 02

What the higher-track rounds test

🧮
Data structures & algorithms. Arrays, strings, hashing, two pointers, sorting, recursion, basic graphs and dynamic programming. This is what separates the bands.
⏱️
Speed + correctness. Timed problems with hidden test cases. Edge cases (empty input, large input) matter.
🧠
Explaining your code. In the interview, expect "why this approach?" and "what's the time complexity?"
🛠️
One real project. Be ready to explain what you built, your part in it and the hardest bug you fixed.
STEP 03

A simple 4-week plan

  • 1
    Week 1: arrays, strings, hashing. 2 easy problems a day.
  • 2
    Week 2: two pointers, sliding window, sorting, binary search.
  • 3
    Week 3: recursion, trees, basic graphs (BFS/DFS).
  • 4
    Week 4: basic DP + 3 full timed mock tests. Practise explaining each solution out loud.
STEP 04

Claude practice prompt

Act as an interviewer for a fresher software role in India (TCS Digital / Infosys Specialist Programmer level). Give me ONE coding problem at that level. Don't show the solution. After I answer: check correctness and edge cases, ask me 2 follow-up questions ("why this approach?", "time complexity?"), then score me out of 10 and tell me the single topic to practise next.

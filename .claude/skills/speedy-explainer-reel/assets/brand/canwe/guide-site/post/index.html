STEP 01

Install the pack

linkedin-agent-skill by Jake Schincariol. Free, MIT-licensed; its README: "no signup, no API key, nothing to connect."

  1. Easiest (Claude app): paste this into Claude:
https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/linkedin-agent-skill Install this skill, then confirm /li-post works.
  1. Claude Code (plugin):

No Claude Code? Paste any single SKILL.md at the top of a chat and it runs as a mode (you lose the humanizer's two Python tools).

STEP 02

The voice file (the part everyone skips)

Every skill reads voice.md. Skip it and your posts sound like everyone else's. Fastest way: paste 3 of your own posts (or 3 messages you wrote) into Claude with this:

Here are 3 things I wrote. Write my voice.md for the linkedin-agent-skill from them: how I open, my sentence length, words I use, words I never use, and how I end a post. Keep it honest; don't make me sound smarter than I am. 1) [paste] 2) [paste] 3) [paste]

Save it as ~/.claude/linkedin/voice.md (the pack's template lives in templates/voice.md).

STEP 03

My 3 prompts

  • 1
    /li-post — "/li-post [one idea from your week: a project, an internship task, a mistake]". You get 3 hooks from its 21 formulas + one full draft, humanized.
  • 2
    /li-profile — scores your profile out of 100 (12-part rubric) and rewrites what lost points, worst first. Do this before you post anything.
  • 3
    /li-plan — your week: what to post, when, and 10 people to engage with. Freshers: pick alumni and recruiters at companies you're applying to.

Bonus: /li-human on anything you wrote with any AI. It strips em dashes, slop words ("leverage", "delve into", "game-changer") and invisible characters, and flags lines like "It's not just X, it's Y".

STEP 04

Post safely

✋
You press post. The pack only writes ("These skills write. You post."). Read every word; it's your name on it.
🚫
No bots on your account. LinkedIn prohibits automated access, so don't connect scrapers or auto-commenters to it. Copy-paste posting has zero risk.
🔗
Want true scheduling? Use an official route (LinkedIn's own scheduler, or a tool that uses LinkedIn's official API such as Zapier's "Create Share Update"), never a browser bot.
📏
Only real numbers. If the draft says "37 interviews", it had better be true.

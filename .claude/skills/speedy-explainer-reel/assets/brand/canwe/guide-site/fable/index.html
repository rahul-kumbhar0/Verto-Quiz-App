STEP 01

Am I eligible? (check in 10 seconds)

  • 1
    Claude Pro or a standard Team seat, with paid billing in place before 9 Nov 2026, 10:00 am PT: Fable 5.2 is included for the week, for up to 50% of your weekly usage limit, plus one Fable limit reset. Both end 16 Nov, 11:59 pm PT.

STEP 02

How to switch to Fable 5.2

Claude app or claude.ai (web, desktop, phone): open a new chat → tap the model picker → choose Fable 5.2.

Claude Code: update first (Fable 5.2 needs version 2.1.321 or later), then switch with the model command:

claude update
/model claude-fable-5-2

Developers on the API: the model id is claude-fable-5-2, priced $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens (Opus 5.5 is $4 / $20).

STEP 03

Fable or Opus? Give Fable your hardest job only

Anthropic's own advice: "Start with Claude Opus 5.5 for most work, and move up to Fable or Mythos for the most ambitious tasks." Fable uses your allowance faster, so spend it where it counts.

⚡
Give Fable: a big build or refactor that keeps failing, a long research or analysis task with many steps, a hard bug you've been stuck on for days, a project plan for something ambitious.
✅
Keep on Opus 5.5 (or Sonnet): emails, summaries, notes, resume edits, everyday questions, small code fixes.
🧪
What it showed at launch (Anthropic, 9 Nov): 1,000+ Fable agents built a prototype operating system in 8 days; ~1,000 agents formalized the proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in 30 hours; it designed 3 devices that worked without electrical fixes.
STEP 04

3 prompts for your hardest task

1 · The stuck project

I've been stuck on this for days. Here is the goal, what I tried, and the errors I got: [paste]. First, list every possible cause, ranked by likelihood, with a quick test for each. Then fix the most likely one and explain what you changed in simple words. Don't change anything outside what's needed.

2 · The big plan

I want to build [project] in [time]. I'm a [student / fresher] who knows [skills]. Make a realistic step-by-step plan: milestones, what to learn at each step, the free tools to use, and the 3 places beginners usually get stuck. Ask me 3 questions first if anything is unclear.

3 · The deep review

Review my [project / code / report] like a strict senior. Find the 10 most important problems, ordered by impact. For each: what's wrong, why it matters, and the exact fix. End with the 3 changes that would improve it the most this week.
STEP 05

Traps to skip

🚫
"Free Fable API key" sites and videos. A shared key or proxy means your prompts, code and files pass through a stranger's server. Not worth it.
🎬
"Free for 72 hours" tool offers. Some apps advertise their own free trial next to the Fable launch. That's their product, not free Fable, and some renew into a paid plan. Read before you sign up.
📅
Mind the time zone. 16 Nov, 11:59 pm PT is Tuesday 17 Nov, 1:29 pm in India.

STEP 01

What actually kills your application

🧢
"75% of resumes are rejected by bots" traces back to a 2012 sales pitch. No study was ever published.
❓
Screening questions. Employers can set rules that auto-reject an application based on your answers (graduation year, sponsorship, location, licences). This is the real auto-reject. Read every question and answer honestly.
🔎
Keyword search. Recruiters search their candidate database for words like Python and SQL. If the exact words from the job post aren't on your resume, you don't show up.
🧩
Layout. Two columns, tables and text boxes can come out scrambled when the resume is parsed. One column is safest.
📥
Volume. An entry-level role can get 400 to 600 applications within days. Applying early and matching the job post closely matter more than any trick.
STEP 02

ATS-safe resume template

Copy it into Google Docs or Word, replace the brackets, keep it to one column, and export as PDF. Use a normal font (Arial, Calibri) and plain headings exactly like these.

[FULL NAME] [City, State] | [phone] | [email] | linkedin.com/in/[you] | github.com/[you] SUMMARY [Final-year B.Tech CSE student / fresher] skilled in [Python, SQL, React]. Built [1 real project with a result]. Looking for a [Software Engineer Intern] role. SKILLS Languages: [Python, Java, SQL] Frameworks & tools: [React, Django, Git, Docker] Concepts: [REST APIs, DBMS, OOP, Data Structures] EXPERIENCE [Role], [Company] | [Month Year] – [Month Year] - [Action verb] [what you built] using [tech], [result with a number] - [Action verb] [what you improved], [result with a number] PROJECTS [Project name] | [Tech used] | [github link] - [What it does, who it helps, one number: users / speed / accuracy] - [The hardest part you solved] EDUCATION [Degree, Branch], [College] | [Year] | CGPA: [x.xx] CERTIFICATIONS & ACHIEVEMENTS - [Certification / hackathon / rank]

✋ No photos, icons, tables, text boxes or skill bars. Put contact details in the body, not in the page header.

STEP 03

The Claude prompt that tailors it

Paste this into Claude, then paste the job post and your resume below it.

You are helping me tailor my resume to one job. Rules: use ONLY facts from my resume, never invent experience, numbers or skills. 1. List the 10 most important keywords and skills in the job post. 2. Show which ones are already in my resume and which are missing. 3. For the missing ones I genuinely have, rewrite my bullet points to include them naturally. 4. For the ones I don't have, tell me honestly and suggest one small project to learn them. 5. Give me the final resume in a single-column, plain-text format with these headings: SUMMARY, SKILLS, EXPERIENCE, PROJECTS, EDUCATION. JOB POST: [paste the job description] MY RESUME: [paste your resume text]
STEP 04

10-second checklist before you apply

  • ✓
    I read every screening question and answered honestly.
  • ✓
    The exact skill words from the job post are in my resume (e.g. "SQL", not just "databases").
  • ✓
    One column, no tables, no text boxes, no images.
  • ✓
    Copy-paste test: when I paste my PDF text into Notes, it reads in the right order.
  • ✓
    File name: Firstname_Lastname_Resume.pdf
  • ✓
    I'm applying in the first few days after the job was posted.

What actually happens when you hit Enter in Claude or ChatGPT:
1️⃣ Your sentence is chopped into tokens (≈ ¾ of a word each)
2️⃣ Every token becomes a list of numbers — AI never reads words
3️⃣ The model scores every possible next token (GPT-2 had 50,257), picks one, and repeats

That's why answers stream in piece by piece — and why the same question can get a different answer. It's predicting, not looking things up.

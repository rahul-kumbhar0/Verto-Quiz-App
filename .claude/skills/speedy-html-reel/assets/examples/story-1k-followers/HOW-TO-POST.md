# 1,000 followers story: how to post (3 slides, in order)

Upload the .mp4 files (the .png files are still versions if you prefer images).

1. **canwe-1k-story-1.mp4** (12 s): counter to 1,000, confetti, "Thank you".
   - Optional: a "🎉" **emoji slider** sticker ("How hyped are you?") in the gap between the text and the presenter.
2. **canwe-1k-story-2.mp4** (10 s): the 4 reels coming this week, with their keywords.
   - Add a **Link sticker** → `https://canwe-guides.vercel.app` with the text "Free guides".
     Place it on the right, under "Free guides here 👇" (the presenter points at it).
3. **canwe-1k-story-3.mp4** (8 s): "You pick the next reel".
   - Add a **Poll sticker** in the empty glowing area in the middle. Question: "Next reel?"
     Poll stickers allow up to 4 options:
     - Free AI tools for placements
     - Coding interview hacks
     - How ChatGPT actually works
     - Side income with AI
   - Keep the bottom 250 px clear (the reply bar covers it). Nothing important is placed there.

After 24 h: read the poll results and tell Claude the winner. It becomes the next reel.
Then post a 4th slide with the result ("You picked X. Dropping Friday.").

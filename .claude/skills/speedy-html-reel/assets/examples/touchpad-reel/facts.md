# Facts + sources (touchpad reel)
- Grid of row + column electrodes; typical grid 16 x 12 (8 x 6 when space is tight) — Cirque-style touchpad patent (US 9,152,284 family; image-ppubs.uspto.gov 9479153 et al.)
- Finger couples away part of the row/column mutual capacitance -> capacitance at that intersection DROPS — MSOE ELE4142 "touch screens capacitive" slides; patents above
- Full-grid scan 20–200 Hz typical (slides); patents up to 200/400/500 Hz
- Resolution "on the order of 960 counts per inch", far finer than the grid (interpolation) — Cirque patent
- Apple Force Touch trackpad (2015): pad does not move; 4 force sensors; Taptic Engine (electromagnets, iFixit) vibrates side to side and the brain reads a click — MacRumors 12 Mar 2015, TechCrunch 12 Mar 2015, Computerworld
- Cirque GlidePoint 1994; Apple PowerBook 500 series launched 16 May 1994 with Cirque GlidePoint trackpad — Wikipedia "Touchpad", applematters.com

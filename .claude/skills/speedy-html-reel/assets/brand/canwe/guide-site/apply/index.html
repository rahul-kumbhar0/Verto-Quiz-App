STEP 01

Give Claude a browser: Playwright (Microsoft, free)

playwright-mcp by Microsoft: 39.1k ⭐, Apache-2.0 (3 Nov 2026). It lets Claude open pages, read forms, type, click and upload files. Needs Node 18+.

Claude Code (one command):

claude mcp add playwright npx @playwright/mcp@latest

Claude Desktop: Settings → Developer → Edit config, add this under mcpServers, restart Claude:

"playwright": { "command": "npx", "args": ["@playwright/mcp@latest"] }

Test it: "Open example.com and tell me the page title." A browser window should open.

STEP 02

Your resume → which jobs you actually fit

This week (7 days to 3 Nov 2026) freehire counted 901 fresher postings in India on company career sites. Don't apply to all of them. Attach your resume (remove your phone number and address first) and paste:

Here is my resume. For each job link I paste, score my fit 0–100 (skills, degree, location, experience asked vs mine), say the 2 biggest gaps honestly, and tell me if it's worth applying. Never invent experience I don't have.

Where to get the links: part 1 of this series (freehire + Claude, guide at /freehire/) or the company's own careers page.

STEP 03

The apply prompt (with the stop rule built in)

Open this application: [paste the careers-page link]. Use my resume (attached) to fill every field: name, email, college, degree, year, projects, and write "why this company" in 3 honest sentences based on my actual projects. Upload resume.pdf where asked. RULE: NEVER click Submit, Apply, or any final button. When every field is filled, STOP, take a screenshot, and show me what you entered so I can check it.
  • 1
    Read the filled form. Fix anything wrong in the browser yourself.
  • 2
    You press Submit. Every time. It's your name on the application.
  • 3
    Next job: paste the next link and the same prompt. Keep a sheet of what you applied to.
STEP 04

The rules (read before your first run)

✋
"Stop before Submit" is a prompt rule, not a setting. Claude follows it well, but check the screenshot every time. Try it first on a form that doesn't matter.
🚫
Company sites only. LinkedIn, Naukri and most job boards prohibit bots and can ban your account. Use Workday / Greenhouse / Lever / the company's own careers page.
📏
No invented experience. The prompt says it; you enforce it. One made-up project costs you the interview.
🔐
Playwright is powerful. Don't enable its "run code unsafe" option, and don't let it log in to your bank or email. It's a browser for forms, not a key to your life.
⏱️
Captchas and OTPs are for you to do by hand. Claude will stop and ask.

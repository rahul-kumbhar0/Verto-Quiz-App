740 jobs. 1 offer. The career-ops setup.

career-ops is a free, open-source job search agent that runs inside Claude Code. It scores every job against your resume before you apply, then drafts the resume, cover letter and form answers for the ones worth it. You still press Submit.

STEP 01

What it does

Its creator ran his own job hunt through it: 740 listings evaluated, 68 applied, 12 interviews, 1 offer (a Head of Applied AI role). Those are his results from the project README, not a promise.

🎯
Scores each job 1–5 against your CV, and pushes you not to apply below 4.0. Fewer, better applications.
📄
Tailored resume PDF for each job worth applying to. It rewords your CV for the role; it must never invent experience.
✉️
Cover letter + form answers drafted for you to review.
🗂️
Tracker of every application, plus interview prep and follow-up modes.
⭐ Open the repo on GitHub
STEP 02

What you need

  1. Node.js on your laptop (nodejs.org, the LTS version is fine).
  2. An AI coding CLI. Claude Code is what I use; it needs a paid Claude plan. It also works with Codex, OpenCode, Qwen and others.
  3. Your resume (any format you can paste) and the roles you want.

💻 It runs on your own computer, not on a website. Your CV and data stay in the folder on your laptop.

STEP 03

Install (one command)

Open a terminal and run:

npx @santifer/career-ops init cd career-ops claude

Use codex (or your CLI) instead of claude if that's what you have.

First launch sets everything up by chat: it asks for your CV, your profile and your target roles. You don't edit any files by hand.

Prefer git? Manual install
git clone https://github.com/career-ops-hq/career-ops.git cd career-ops && npm install npx playwright install chromium claude

The Playwright step is only needed for PDF generation.

STEP 04

Score your first job

Inside Claude Code, just paste a job link (or the job description text). That runs the full pipeline: score, report, tailored PDF.

Or use the commands:

/career-ops oferta

Evaluation only (score + report, no PDF). Paste the job after it.

/career-ops

Shows every command: pdf, cover, tracker, scan, interview-prep and more.

Where things are saved: reports in reports/, PDFs in output/, your tracker in data/applications.md.

STEP 05

Reading the score

🟢
4.0 and above: worth applying. Generate the PDF, read it fully, fix anything wrong, then apply yourself.
🔴
Below 4.0: it recommends skipping. You can override it, but that's the point: stop spending applications on roles you're unlikely to get.
🧭
Read the report, not just the number. It tells you what's missing, which is your learning list.
STEP 06

Rules + good to know

✋
It never submits. It drafts; you review every CV and answer and press Submit. That's by design.
🚫
No fake experience. AI can hallucinate skills. Delete anything that isn't true before you send.
📨
Don't spam. The README itself says not to use it to flood employers. Quality over volume.
🇮🇳
Indian companies? Its scanner comes set up for 100+ mostly global tech companies. For Indian roles, paste links from LinkedIn, Naukri or company career pages directly; the scoring works on any job you paste.

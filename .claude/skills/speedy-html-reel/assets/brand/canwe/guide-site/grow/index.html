STEP 03

My exact workflow (what made this account)

  • 1
    Pick the topic with a scorecard, not a mood. A topic must hit 5 of 6: a chore everyone has · "someone automated it" story · a free named tool · showable in 5 seconds · exact numbers · fresh news or evergreen pain.
  • 2
    Script before anything. Write 10 hooks, score them, keep the best. One idea per sentence, 6–14 words. Every number must have a source you actually read.
  • 3
    Fast delivery with hard stops. Dense speech, then half a second of full silence right on the twist line. 4–6 stops per reel.
  • 4
    One keyword per reel. "Comment WORD" → an auto-DM with a link to a free guide page like this one. The DM must deliver exactly what the reel promised.
  • 5
    Log every result. Views, comments, follows per reel go into a table; the next topic copies what beat the average.

Prompt to steal — paste into Claude with the pack installed:

STEP 04

The honest rules (why this works)

✋
AI never presses post. Read every word before it goes up. You're responsible for what your account says.
📏
Never invent numbers. Every stat on screen needs a source. One made-up number costs more followers than ten good reels gain.
🚫
No comment spam, no engagement bait. Skills that write comments are for genuine replies, not mass flattery on strangers' posts.
🏷️
Disclose paid posts. In India, ASCI rules require a clear "paid partnership" label on sponsored content.
⏳
Results take reps. This account posted daily with keyword CTAs from day one. The pack writes; the consistency is still on you.

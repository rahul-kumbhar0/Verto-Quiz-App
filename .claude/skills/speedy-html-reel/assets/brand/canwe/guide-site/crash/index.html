STEP 02

Why (two words: election + bubble)

Coverage ties the selloff to two things at once: the US midterm election results (markets fear policy changes around AI infrastructure spending), and a growing fear that the massive AI buildout — chips, data centres, energy — is a bubble: more money going in than the revenue coming out so far. No single company reported bad news; the mood changed.

📅 The next big test is Nvidia's earnings on 17 November. Analysts want revenue at or above the ~$108B guidance. We'll cover it — follow for part 2.

STEP 03

A stock price is a mood. A job posting is a decision.

A stock price moves because millions of people change their feelings about the future, in seconds. A job posting exists because a manager got a budget approved, wrote a description, and committed to pay a salary for a year — a slow, deliberate decision. They're connected (a long funding winter does slow hiring), but they are not the same thing, and they don't move on the same day.

So "AI stocks fell 9%" does not mean "AI jobs fell 9%". It means investors got scared. Whether hiring changes shows up months later — in the postings data, not the ticker.

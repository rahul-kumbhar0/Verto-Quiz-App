## The 3 beats (≈ 3 s, ≤ 18 words)
1. **The pain / the claim, 2–6 words, with a number or a hard fact.** "Placement season. Zero offers." ·
   "Stop paying for editing tutorials." · "I applied to 200 jobs. Zero replies."
2. **The twist with Claude, 4–7 words.** "So I let Claude apply." · "Claude made this reel."
3. **The promise, 3–5 words.** "Here's every step." · "Here's how you can too." (opens the loop; the CTA closes it)

On screen (speedy-explainer-reel): beat 1 = the BIG frame-0 headline (`M.headline`, readable muted) over the
blurred proof; beat 2 = the proof comes forward; beat 3 = the step tiles deal in. The presenter is already on
screen at frame 0 (`avatar.in:-0.5`), reacting on beat 1 (shrug / facepalm / stop), pointing on beat 3.

## Voice on the opening (see voice.md § Dynamics)
Beat 1 `[matter of fact]` or `[curious]`, flat and low: the pain is said, not sold. Beat 2 `<up>` + a `^stressed^`
word (the twist is the first lift). `<short pause>` before beat 3; beat 3 back to normal, warm. The first laugh,
if any, never before beat 2.

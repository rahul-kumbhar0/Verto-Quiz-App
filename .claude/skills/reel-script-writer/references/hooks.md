# Hook formulas (the first 1.5 seconds)

Every hook has two layers: the **spoken line** (≤ 12 words, payoff noun in the first 6) and the
**frame-1 on-screen text** (≤ 7 words, readable before the voice finishes). Frame 1 must already
SHOW something (the demo mid-action, the object, a number in the selection box), never a blank
title card. Write 10 per script from ≥ 5 formulas, score them (SKILL.md §2), keep 3.

## Hook rules
- Payoff noun early: "I automated my **job search**…" not "So today I want to tell you about…".
- Never open with a greeting, the channel name, "in this video", or a question nobody asked
  ("Have you ever wondered…?" scores 0 on Specific).
- A number beats an adjective: "3 million live jobs", not "tons of jobs".
- Name the brand (with its real logo on screen): Claude, ChatGPT, LinkedIn, TCS. Brands are search
  terms and pattern-interrupts.
- The hook opens a loop the next 5 s pays off partly (the demo starts) but the full payoff is held
  for the end ("…and the best part is what it does when you open a job").
- The frame-1 text can be the shorter, punchier version: spoken "I basically automated my entire
  job search", frame text "I automated my job search 🤖" with *job search* in the selection box.
- Never write a hook we can't prove on screen. "True" = 0 deletes it.

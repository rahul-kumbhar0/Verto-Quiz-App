# What the winning reels do (analysis, 27 Oct 2026)

Method: PullRows `trends_find` on Instagram, 30 posts scanned per query, ranked by momentum (views
per day) + engagement, top 10 kept, for **#aitools, #claudeai, #jobsearch, #techexplained**
(40 posts, 37 unique). View counts are the tool's estimates; likes/comments are exact.
Instagram does not publish spoken transcripts, so hooks = the first caption line / on-screen line,
and many creators paste the full script into the caption (used below). Re-run the scans every
4–6 weeks: formats drift fast (median winner age was 11–21 days).

## The headline findings
1. **Two different games.** Reels that end with a comment keyword ("Comment X and I'll DM you…")
   collect comments at **0.3–2.2× their likes**. Pure explainers collect **~1 comment per 250–350
   likes** but the biggest reach.

   → For leads, end with a keyword. For reach, explain something people are curious about and
   end with a save/share/question. **Our hybrid** (explainer body + keyword CTA) aims for both:
   the freehire reel did 31K views and 613 comments (~2% of views) in 2 days.
2. **Original voice wins.** 7–9 of 10 winners in every niche used original audio (voice-over or
   talking), not a trending track. Our voice-over format fits.
3. **Proof beats promise.** The strongest keyword reels SHOW the thing working ("I turned Claude
   into a trading research team with 11 specialized agents", "One clip. 7 completely different
   edits. Each one is a single /effects command") or give a hard result ("10 days. 3 offers. No
   referral requests."). Weak ones only promise ("this will change your life").
4. **Freshness is a hook.** "Someone just open-sourced…", "Google has silently released 15 AI
   tools…", "Claude Just Destroyed Instagram With 13 Free Skills", "Free Claude Max Subscription":
   new + free + named brand. Winners were posted within the last 1–3 weeks.
5. **Explainers win with a paradox or a hidden structure**: "Nobody ever wrote the code that lets
   ChatGPT write Python. Not one line of it."; "Spotify feels like one app. It's really six
   machines"; "A 1 GB folder zips down to 400 MB and unzips back to the full 1 GB". Then a
   concrete worked example (BANANA in 48 bits → 9 bits) and exact numbers.
6. **Series framing** ("explain it like I'm five, part 1", "Part 60", "1/3") gives a reason to
   follow and comment for the next part.
7. **Hinglish works for Indian reach**: rajenlabs "bhai SCREENSHOT le lo, ye 11 Free AI Tools
   tumhare career ke end tak kaam aayenge" (≈36M est. views, 1.44M likes): save-bait + Hinglish +
   list + career stakes. aiandharshit "Soch raha hai poori Instagram team FREE mein mil jaye?"
8. **Timely opportunity posts** get huge comment ratios without a demo: careerlaunch_in "Wipro
   Direct interview on 27th and 28th Oct… 2021–2026 batches eligible. Comment below" → 3.7K likes,
   17K comments in 24 h. (Only post real, verified openings.)
9. **Relatable memes** reach big but convert poorly to comments (kchbhi9060 recruiter meme: 80K
   likes, 244 comments). Use as an occasional reach post, never as a lead post.

## Captions (what the winners write under the reel)
- **Line 1 = the hook or the keyword line** ("Comment “TRADE” and I’ll send you the full setup 👇").
- Median length of winners: **66–115 words** (longer than the losers in 3 of 4 niches): a numbered
  or arrowed breakdown of the value, so the caption itself is saveable.
- **Search keyword block**: many winners append `[keywords: Claude, Claude AI, Claude skills, …]`
  or a plain list of search phrases ("job search tips, free resume builder, AI interview prep…").
  Instagram search reads captions; this is cheap reach.
- **3–5 hashtags**, niche-first (#claudeai #aitools #jobsearch), not 30.
- **Honesty/credit lines** build trust: "Not sponsored", "from its own README", "The catch: it never
  presses Submit", "You need Claude Pro (₹2,000/month annual)". nocodealex and shashwat___agarwal
  both state the limitation before the CTA.
- **Follow gate stated**: "Follow so the DM lands 📩", "Full setup sirf followers ko DM me milega".

## Endings seen on winners
- Keyword DM: "Comment APPLY for the repo link." (repeated at the end of the caption too)
- Save for a specific future moment: "Save this for your next system design interview."; "Save
  this for the next time someone calls AI 'just autocomplete.'"
- Share to a specific friend: "send it to a friend who still pays for Claude"; "send it to a
  friend who's applying right now".
- Easy question: "Which step surprised you most? Drop the number 👇"; "Did you think zip deleted
  something to save space? Yes or no in the comments."; "which tech word should Claude explain
  like you're five next?"

## Posting time seen
Winners clustered around 19:00–22:00 local time (the scan reported Tue/Thu/Fri/Sun peaks). Our
default: 19:00–21:00 IST.

## The always-viral formula (validated 28 Oct 2026, fresh scans + our own results)
**CHORE x CLAUDE x FREE**: a chore/pain nearly everyone in the audience has -> "I/someone automated
it with Claude" -> named FREE tool (a GitHub repo = trust) -> exact result numbers -> comment keyword.
Evidence (views): TV-ads chore 2.9M in 4 days (cleanyourtv, 43.1k stars) / job search 275K + our 40K
(freehire) / token limits 675K Hinglish with 59K comments / trading 1.4M / Instagram posting 430K.
Score a topic 0-6 before writing (build only 5+):
1. Universal pain: >= 80% of OUR audience fights it this month (TV ads, job hunt, WhatsApp overload, fees)
2. Automation arc: "I gave Claude X and it did my chore" (first person beats listicle)
3. Free + named: a real repo/tool with stars; "free" said early
4. Showable in 5 s: a before/after or live demo frame 1 can show
5. Numbers as proof: stars, counts, money, days ("740 jobs. 1 offer.")
6. Fresh (<= 7 days old news) OR evergreen pain; median winner age in scans = 3 days
Delivery that goes with it: original voiceover (100% of winners), comment-keyword CTA, caption
~100+ words with a [keywords:...] block, and stop-and-go pacing (voice.md, Pacing section).
